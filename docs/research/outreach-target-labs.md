# Annulus AI — Outreach Target Labs

**Date:** 2026-06-24
**Institutions:** Stanford · Columbia · University of Utah (~25 labs each)

**Target profile:** labs that (1) generate lots of tabular/spreadsheet experimental data, (2) are sensitive about data privacy/IP (clinical, patient, or proprietary data), and (3) have non-programmer members who currently wrangle data in Excel. **Tier 1** = lead with these (privacy pitch lands hardest). **Tier 2** = good fit, pitch the "Excel replacement for non-programmers" angle.

> **Verify before sending.** Every PI and URL below was found via live web research, but: (a) `medicine.utah.edu` / `uofuhealth.utah.edu` and a few Stanford/Columbia pages block automated fetchers (HTTP 403 ≠ dead link), (b) a handful of links are faculty-profile or division pages rather than dedicated lab sites, and (c) some senior PIs should be confirmed as actively running a lab. Do a 30-second manual check of each name, title, and page before emailing. Inline flags note the specific ones.

## How to prioritize

1. **Lead with a Tier-1 lab at each school**, ideally one where the team already has a connection (warm intros convert far better than cold).
2. **Strongest privacy-pitch targets** = anyone handling patient/clinical/proprietary data (most Tier 1 here). Lead the email with the privacy/IP angle for those.
3. **Personalize the one "why this is relevant to you" sentence** per lab — pull it from the "Why it fits" column.
4. **Start with ~3–5 per school**, measure reply rate, then work down the list.
