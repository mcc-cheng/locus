## Tips

- **Personalize only that one sentence.** It's the difference between a reply and the trash — pull it from the target-labs sheet's "Why it fits" column.
- **Keep it to one screen.** Resist adding a second paragraph about features; the goal is a yes/no, not a full pitch.
- **Make the ask tiny.** "15-minute call" + an explicit easy out ("just reply 'not now'") lowers the cost of replying.
- **Use a warm intro when you have one** — put it in the first sentence ([MUTUAL CONNECTION]).
